# Source investigation and simulation provenance
Access date: 17 October 2026.

- AISHE official site: https://aishe.gov.in/ describes the annual higher-education survey. Used only to identify the relevant public-data source, not as a commercial pipeline dataset.
- Official institution directory: https://dashboard.aishe.gov.in/hedirectory . Search found the directory; direct retrieval returned 502 during this session. No institution records imported.
- AISHE website policies: https://aishe.gov.in/website-policies/ . Policy discusses copyright and permission for reproduction. No AISHE data is redistributed.
- Government Open Data License India: https://data.gov.in/Godl . Candidate reuse framework requiring attribution for covered datasets. No suitable institution-level extract was acquired or incorporated. This does not license arbitrary AISHE pages.

The entire delivered dataset is synthetic, seed 20261017. Real state/city names provide geographic context only. Names begin Simulated and are not assertions about actual institutions. CampusBridge Demo and representatives are fictional. No actual KollegeApply clients, market share, revenue, contact details or performance are used. This is not an endorsed KollegeApply project.

Generator: 600 institutions, one opportunity each; random creation dates across 1 Oct 2025–30 Sep 2026; forward-only stage entries, explicit lost branches, and snapshot censoring. Stage progression can stop, producing long-stalled leads intentionally. Activity dates remain between lead creation and closure/snapshot. Contracts exist only for wins. Service accrual is daily, 365-day term. Forecast probabilities are planning assumptions, not fitted win frequencies. No external market-size claims are derived from this simulation.
